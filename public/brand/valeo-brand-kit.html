01 — Brand Overview

Who We Are

The Valeo Experience is a Caribbean mental health platform connecting clients with Dr. Jozelle M. Miller, PhD. Our brand communicates growth, warmth, and grounded expertise — rooted in the Caribbean, relevant to the world.

Growth

The lime green leaves represent personal growth and flourishing — the journey toward wellbeing.

Warmth

The orange teardrop symbolises empathy, presence, and the human connection at the heart of every session.

Movement

The swoosh arcs convey motion, progress, and the dynamic energy of transformation.

02 — Logo System

Logo Variants

The logo has three components: the Logomark (icon), the Wordmark (typography), and the Lockup (combined). Each is available in multiple configurations to suit every use case.

Primary Stacked Lockup

Primary — Light Background

Default version. Use on white, ivory, or light surfaces.

Primary — General Use

Full-colour version for all standard applications — web, documents, presentations.

On Tint Background

Works well on the leaf (#F2F8EA) or any light neutral surface.

On Dark — Boxed

On dark backgrounds, place the logo in a white container. A true reversed (all-white) version requires a designer to produce a vector source file.

Horizontal Lockup

Horizontal — Light Background

Use in headers, email footers, and anywhere with limited vertical space. A horizontal crop can be produced from the source file.

Horizontal — Dark Background

Nav bars, dark footers, slide decks. Use the logo in a white container until a reversed vector version is available.

Logomark & Wordmark

Mark — Social / Profile

Avatars, social media profiles, community platforms. Use the square 400×400 or 800×800.

Mark — App Icon

PWA, Android, iOS home screen. Available at 192, 512, and 1024px.

Mark — Favicon

Browser tab icon. Available at 16, 32, 48, 64, 128, and 256px. Auto-wired to the site.

Single Colour Versions

Note: Monochrome (single-colour) and reversed (all-white) logo variants require the original vector source file to produce correctly. These should be commissioned from a graphic designer when needed for print, merchandise, or single-ink applications.

Greyscale (Reference Only)

This is a CSS greyscale preview for reference. A proper monochrome version requires vector artwork.

On Dark — Boxed (Reference Only)

Place the logo in a white container on dark backgrounds until a reversed white-only vector is available.

Clear Space

Always maintain a minimum clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "E" in EXPERIENCE. This protects the logo from visual clutter and ensures legibility at all sizes.

Minimum size: The full stacked lockup should never appear smaller than 120px wide in digital use or 25mm in print. The mark alone should never appear smaller than 24px.

06 — Usage Rules

Do's & Don'ts

Consistent application of the logo protects brand integrity. These rules apply across all media.

✓ Do

✓
Use the full-colour primary version on white, ivory, and light backgrounds.
✓
Use the white reversed version on forest green, charcoal, or photographic backgrounds.
✓
Maintain the required clear space around the logo at all times.
✓
Use the horizontal lockup in headers, email footers, and narrow spaces.
✓
Use the PNG files provided in this kit for all digital applications. Source all exports from Original Logo.jpg.
✓
Scale the logo proportionally — always maintain the original aspect ratio.
✓
Use the monochrome version for single-colour print situations only.

✗ Don't

✗
Do not change any of the logo colours or apply unapproved tints.
✗
Do not stretch, squish, or distort the logo in any direction.
✗
Do not add drop shadows, outlines, or effects to the logo.
✗
Do not place the colour logo on a busy photographic background — use the reversed (white) version instead.
✗
Do not rotate or tilt the logo.
✗
Do not recreate or redraw the logo — always use the approved files.
✗
Do not place the logo on backgrounds with insufficient contrast.
